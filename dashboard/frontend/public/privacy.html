Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026 · Effective immediately for Betterverse & 100B Automated Pipeline

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Betterverse ("we", "our", or "the Service"), operating the 100B Automated YouTube Shorts curation and broadcasting system, accesses, stores, and protects user data and YouTube API credentials.

We strictly adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

2. Google & YouTube API Data We Access

When you authorize Betterverse via Google OAuth 2.0, our application requests only the minimum necessary scopes:

  • youtube.upload: Used solely to upload scheduled video shorts to your authorized YouTube channel.
  • youtube.readonly: Used to inspect publish status, view metrics, and Content ID claim status.
  • youtube: Used to manage scheduled broadcast publish times and video descriptions.

3. How Data Is Stored & Protected

Local Security Architecture: All Google OAuth tokens are stored exclusively on the operator's private PC in secured local directories. OAuth tokens are never transmitted to third parties, never uploaded to Vercel, and never stored in cloud databases.

4. Zero Data Sharing & Limited Use

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transfer your Google user data. No data is used to train machine learning models, and no advertising cookies are used.

5. Revoking Access & Data Deletion

You can revoke access to your YouTube channel at any time via Google Account Security Permissions. Locally stored credentials can be removed by deleting the uploader/secrets/ directory.

6. Contact

For questions or support, contact the developer at github.com/mrmorningstar-dev/100B.
